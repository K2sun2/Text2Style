Text2Style: Text-Derived Style Encoding and
Instruction-Guided Emotion Editing for Expressive TTS

[Audio Samples]

  • Text-Derived Default Synthesis
  • Instruction-Guided Emotion Editing
  • Quantitative Results

Abstract: Instruction-conditioned TTS typically uses instructions as direct generation conditions, without explicitly modeling a text-derived default style state. We propose Text2Style, a three-stage Encoding–Editing–Realization framework that learns a style state from text and edits it under optional emotion instructions. Stage I learns the state through functional-response distillation, matching responses induced by text-derived and acoustic-derived style controls in a frozen text-to-semantic (T2S) module. Stage II trains state-conditioned residual edits with multi-start objectives for target-style acquisition, conflict correction, and repeated-instruction control consistency. Stage III reuses the state to condition the T2S and Flow modules for speech generation. On held-out TextrolSpeech and LIME-Core splits, Text2Style achieves E-SIM/WER of 0.8643/1.38% and 0.8736/1.50% in text-derived default synthesis and synthesis with emotion instructions, respectively, with the highest mean nMOS and eMOS among the evaluated systems.
